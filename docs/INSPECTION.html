Owner support

Bright pixels are small semantic fragments or parent-silhouette tails found in the sharp owner frame. Satellites must improve absolute physical fit; parent silhouettes require strong containment plus a material relative fit gain inside the fragment’s optical influence neighborhood. These pixels hard-select observed foreground.

Front reconstruction

Bright pixels are safely inside a forward-winning focused-owner silhouette and predicted by both PSFs to be mixed in the far-focus frame. They hard-select the directly observed front layer before any rear recovery.

Ordered visibility

Metric dictionary

What the numbers do—and do not—claim

ΔSSIM
Output SSIM minus base SSIM. Positive is better, but SSIM can underweight localized physical mistakes.
ΔMAE / ΔMSE
Output error minus base error against GT. Negative is better; these direct errors prevent a structural metric from carrying the verdict alone.
ΔPSNR
PSNR improvement derived from MSE. Positive is better.
Fringe MAE
Per-channel absolute error inside the GT-defined defocus fringe. This is oracle-localized and cannot be measured on ordinary captures.
False texture
GT-credited finest-band error on smooth true-background fringe pixels. Positive delta is a warning even when global metrics improve.
Forward ratio
Observation-domain residual after/before the 512px physical fit. Lower is better; the license requires <0.85, but forward fit alone is not truth.
Owner-support fit Δ
Absolute reduction in the same observation-domain residual after adding owner-only semantic support. Satellites require more than 0.01 globally; high-overlap parent silhouettes additionally require more than 5% improvement inside the proposed fragment’s PSF-dilated optical neighborhood. GT is not used.
Stable fraction
Fraction of RGB components with matching correction sign across three regularizers and two plausible PSFs. It is consensus, not correctness.
Changed closer/worse
GT class of each changed pixel. Counts expose whether a small global win hides a spatially coherent regression.
α IoU / precision / recall
GT-only segmentation audit of the selected semantic matte. Low recall can reveal missed foreground; low precision can reveal leaks.
